Independent by design

Specialized attention. Enterprise discipline.

Secure Asset Intelligence Services is led by Stacy Serrantino, an independent public-records researcher and systems builder. The work is made for partners who need depth without adding another employee, another subscription, or another layer of noise.

Accountability

Work is performed and reviewed by Stacy Serrantino. Tools may accelerate discovery; they do not issue findings.

Jurisdictions are treated as distinct source maps. A county is not assumed to work like its neighbor.

When to bring SAS in

  • A promising lead needs proof before more resources are spent.
  • A team needs research capacity without hiring another employee.
  • The record trail is fragmented and needs a sourced timeline.
