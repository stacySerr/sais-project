Surplus-funds intelligence

Proof before commitment.

The question is what the record shows about the sale, any deposited proceeds, the last owner, and open recorded interests — not whether a claim should be filed.

Typical research questions

  • Was a tax sale completed, and what official source confirms it?
  • Do records show proceeds deposited, held, or already distributed?
  • Who is the last owner of record, and are there name or entity conflicts?
  • What liens, judgments, or other interests remain visible in the record?
  • What still cannot be verified from available official sources?

Hard limits

SAS does not determine legal eligibility, represent claimants, file claims, send demands, or receive funds.

Attorneys and authorized professionals retain responsibility for advice, filings, contracts, and compliance.

How surplus work is packaged

Findings are delivered as a sourced memo with a timeline, source log, open conflicts, and a recommended next research action.
