What a research memo looks like.

Structure only. No live matters, parcel identifiers, or claim amounts are published here.

  1. Matter identifier and jurisdiction
  2. Question asked
  3. Records searched (office + date searched)
  4. Sale / tax / court events established
  5. Last owner and recorded interests
  6. Open conflicts and name or entity issues
  7. What could not be verified
  8. Confidence notes
  9. Recommended next research action
  10. Source log

Source log columns

  • Repository / office
  • Instrument or record ID
  • Date of record
  • Date captured
  • Citation or official path
  • What it establishes
  • Confidence / limits
