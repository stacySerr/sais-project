Operating principles

Precision is the product.

Speed matters only when the work remains defensible. SAS is designed around disciplined research, explicit uncertainty, and responsible handling of sensitive information.

Source

Official record, repository, instrument, and capture date travel with the finding.

Verified

Material points are checked against more than one official path when available.

Human

Automation may locate. A person reviews before a finding is issued.

Actionable

The output says what was established, what was not, and what to do next in research terms — not filing terms.

Gap

Missing records, closed offices, name variants, and unresolved conflicts are listed, not hidden.

Scope

Facts, inferences, gaps, and next actions are clearly separated.

Clear boundaries

SAS does not provide legal advice, represent claimants, file claims, receive client funds, or make unreviewed legal eligibility determinations.
