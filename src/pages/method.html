The method

A visible chain from source to conclusion.

Good research is not a pile of links. It is a controlled process that shows what was found, how it was tested, and what still needs to be resolved.

01 · Locate

Identify the controlling jurisdiction, official sources, and relevant record set — not a secondary aggregator.

02 · Verify

Cross-check parties, property, amounts, dates, and legal instruments against primary sources.

03 · Trace

Reconstruct ownership, encumbrances, events, and unresolved conflicts. Do not smooth over contradictions.

04 · Deliver

Package the evidence, confidence, gaps, and recommended next research action as separate statements.

If a fact cannot be sourced, it is not stated as a fact.

Source maps are built county by county. Coverage is not treated as interchangeable. Automation may accelerate discovery; a person still reviews material findings.
