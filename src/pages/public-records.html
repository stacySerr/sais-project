Public-records research

Primary sources, preserved and cited.

Targeted retrieval across courts, tax offices, land records, and government repositories. The work is retrieval plus analysis — not a pile of links.

What this page is for

Use this service when the record trail is fragmented and you need a coherent, sourced account of what official offices actually show.

  • Court dockets and judgments
  • Land records and recorded instruments
  • Tax sale, assessment, and related tax-office records
  • Probate, heir, and entity-status records when relevant
  • Government notices tied to the property or parties

Scope we set first

  1. Controlling jurisdiction
  2. Research question in one sentence
  3. Offices and record classes to search
  4. Date range and name variants
  5. Deliverable format and deadline

What leaves with the work

Each material finding carries source, capture date, method, and confidence. Inferences are labeled. Gaps stay visible.
